# 🚀 Learning Journey: WorldQuant BRAIN Simulation Tutorial

I am currently studying the **WorldQuant BRAIN Simulation Tutorial**.  
This platform is designed to help aspiring quants **learn, earn, and grow** in the field of quantitative finance by building and testing trading signals.

---

## 🎯 Purpose
The goal of this tutorial is to:
- Understand the fundamentals of quantitative research.
- Learn how to design predictive signals using financial data.
- Practice simulation and backtesting of strategies.
- Build a foundation for contributing to WorldQuant’s crowdsourced research ecosystem.

---

## 📚 What I Will Learn in Depth

### 1. Introduction to Quantitative Finance
- Basics of quantitative trading and signal design.
- How data drives decision-making in financial markets.

### 2. Signal Creation
- Defining signals from raw financial data.
- Mathematical and statistical techniques for signal generation.
- Importance of normalization and scaling.

### 3. Simulation & Backtesting
- Running simulations to test signal performance.
- Evaluating signals against benchmarks.
- Understanding overfitting and robustness in models.

### 4. Performance Metrics
- Key evaluation criteria: correlation, Sharpe ratio, risk-adjusted returns.
- How to interpret simulation results.
- Identifying strong vs. weak signals.

### 5. Practical Application
- Submitting signals to the WorldQuant BRAIN platform.
- Earning points and recognition through contributions.
- Building a portfolio of signals for long-term growth.
