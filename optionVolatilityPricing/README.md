# 📘 Learning Journey: Option Pricing and Volatility

I am currently studying **Option Pricing and Volatility: Advanced Strategies and Trading Techniques** by *Sheldon Natenberg (McGraw-Hill, 1994)*.  
This book is a cornerstone in professional options trading, bridging theory with practical strategies used by market makers and institutional traders.

---

## 🎯 Purpose
The goal of this reading is to:
- Build a deep understanding of how options are priced.
- Master volatility as the central concept in options trading.
- Develop professional-level risk management and hedging skills.
- Translate theoretical models into actionable trading techniques.

---

## 📚 What I Will Learn in Depth

### 1. Foundations of Option Pricing
- How options derive value from underlying assets.
- The role of time decay, interest rates, and volatility.
- Black-Scholes and other pricing models.

### 2. Volatility as the Core Variable
- Historical vs. implied volatility.
- How volatility drives option premiums.
- Identifying mispriced options through volatility analysis.

### 3. The Greeks and Dynamic Risk Management
- Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta: what they mean and how to use them.
- Position adjustments and hedging techniques.
- Managing risk dynamically rather than statically.

### 4. Advanced Trading Strategies
- Spreads, straddles, strangles, and butterflies.
- Volatility trading vs. directional trading.
- Arbitrage opportunities and professional-style execution.

### 5. Professional Mindset
- Thinking like a market maker.
- Focusing on risk/reward balance instead of prediction.
- Discipline, adaptability, and exploiting inefficiencies.

---

## 🛠️ Practical Outcomes
By the end of this study, I aim to:
- Confidently analyze option positions using volatility and Greeks.
- Construct hedged strategies that balance risk and reward.
- Apply professional trading techniques to real-world markets.
- Document insights and strategies for future reference.
